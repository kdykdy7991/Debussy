What's our data-retention policy for customer PII, and does it differ by region?
Consulted the policy index — 3 documents reading
Assistant · cited 3 sources

Your baseline retention ceiling is 24 months after the commercial relationship ends, unless a legal hold applies — regions with stricter law override it downward.

Two regions matter most. For EMEA, GDPR erasure obligations bring effective retention closer to 12 months for marketing-adjacent PII, and require a documented legal basis for any longer hold.[1] For APAC, the 24-month standard holds globally, but Singapore and South Korea carry shorter per-category windows for financial and health data.[2]

✎

Heads up: the EMEA addendum is a living document (revised six weeks ago), so the 12-month figure is current-but-moving. A legal-hold check found no active holds right now.[3]

The master policy is owned by Legal & Privacy. The EMEA addendum is the one most likely to shift next quarter — worth a re-check before you rely on it externally.
